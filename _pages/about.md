I am a Ph.D. candidate in Data Science at Academy for Advanced Interdisciplinary Studies, Peking University, advised by Prof. [Tiejun Li](https://math.pku.edu.cn/teachers/litj/). Prior to this, I received my B.S. degree from the School of Mathematical Sciences, at Fudan University in 2021, majoring in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics. My research interests lie in generative models and sampling algorithms from a machine learning perspective.
